Baluarte de San Andres

Baluarte de San Andres is a historical site that offers visitors a glimpse into the rich history and heritage of place. Although it is known for its old structures and historical significance it can also be great spot for visitors who want to explore, take photos, and enjoy a quiet moment. As my favorite spot in Manila, I especially enjoyed how cool and refreshing the breeze was, making the place feel peaceful and relaxing despite being a historical site.

Place Details

Address Located in Intramuros, Manila, Philippines
Opening Hours Monday - Sunday: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Entrance Fee Free
Best Time to Visit Morning and Late Afternoon
